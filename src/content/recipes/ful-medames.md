A thick, refried-bean-ish ful medames made with canned fava beans, cumin, Aleppo pepper, tomato, lime, and a bright tomato-cucumber salad on top.

## Ingredients

### Tomato-cucumber salad

- [ ] 1/3 cup tomato, finely diced
- [ ] 1/3 cup cucumber, finely diced
- [ ] 1/3 cup red onion, finely diced
- [ ] Small handful parsley, chopped
- [ ] Juice of 1 lemon
- [ ] Olive oil, roughly equal to the lemon juice
- [ ] Big pinch salt
- [ ] Black pepper

### Beans

- [ ] 2 cans fava beans, drained and rinsed
- [ ] 2 tablespoons olive oil
- [ ] 1/2 cup onion, very finely minced
- [ ] 2-3 garlic cloves, minced
- [ ] 1 teaspoon Aleppo pepper
- [ ] 1 tablespoon ground cumin
- [ ] 1/2 cup tomato, finely chopped
- [ ] 1/2 cup water, plus more as needed
- [ ] Juice of 1/2 lime, plus more to taste
- [ ] Salt, to taste

### To serve

- [ ] Toasted flatbread or pita

## Instructions

1. Mix the **tomato, cucumber, red onion, parsley, lemon juice, olive oil, salt, and black pepper** for the salad. Leave it to marinate while you make the beans.
2. Heat the **olive oil** in a large sauté pan over medium heat.
3. Add the **minced onion** and sweat until translucent around the edges.
4. Add the **garlic, Aleppo pepper, and cumin**. Cook for **30-60 seconds**, just to bloom the spices.
5. Add the **chopped tomato** and drained **fava beans**.
6. Add **1/2 cup water**.
7. Mash the beans as they cook. Aim for a **thick, refried-bean-ish texture**, not completely smooth.
8. Cook for **15-20 minutes**, letting the beans soften and the flavors meld. Add a splash of water if it gets too dry.
9. Stir in the **lime juice**. Taste and add more lime and salt as needed.
10. Spoon into a bowl and pile the marinated tomato-cucumber salad on top.
11. Serve with **toasted flatbread or pita**.

## Notes

- The garlic quantity was cut off in the screenshots; 2-3 cloves is a practical starting point.

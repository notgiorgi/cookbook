A loaded focaccia topped with spring onions, broccoli, zucchini, sausage, mozzarella, and pecorino romano.

## Ingredients

### Dough
- [ ] 500 g AP flour
- [ ] 100 g whole wheat flour
- [ ] 2 tsp instant yeast
- [ ] 10 g salt
- [ ] 480 g water

### Toppings
- [ ] 3 small spring onions
- [ ] 1 small head broccoli
- [ ] 2 small zucchini
- [ ] 1 tbsp olive oil
- [ ] 1 tsp salt
- [ ] 1/4 lb sausage meat
- [ ] 1 cup shredded mozzarella
- [ ] 1/4 cup pecorino romano, grated

## Instructions
1. Add all the dough ingredients to a large mixing bowl and mix with a bread whisk until just combined and the dough comes together in a ball.
2. Place a moist towel over the bowl and let rest for a few hours, or until the dough doubles in size.
3. Slice the vegetables into similarly sized thin pieces so they cook evenly and completely when baking.
4. Add the vegetables to a large mixing bowl, season with the olive oil and salt, and mix.
5. Remove the towel from the dough. With wet hands, fold the dough over a few times into a smooth ball.
6. Generously oil the surface of a large baking pan so the dough will not stick.
7. Dump the dough into the pan, press down lightly, then cover again with the moist towel until it spreads out into the whole pan, about 1 to 2 hours.
8. Spread the sausage meat evenly over the dough, followed by the seasoned vegetables.
9. Using your fingers, press the vegetables and meat into the dough.
10. Top with the shredded mozzarella and bake at 450°F for 20 minutes.
11. Add the grated pecorino and let cool completely before removing from the pan or slicing.
12. Remove from the pan, slice, and enjoy.

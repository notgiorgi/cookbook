A simple red lentil dal with ghee, curry leaves, warm spices, tomato, lemon, and cilantro.

## Ingredients

- [ ] 2 tablespoons ghee or butter
- [ ] 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
- [ ] 6-10 curry leaves
- [ ] 1 medium onion, finely chopped
- [ ] 3 garlic cloves, minced
- [ ] 1 tablespoon fresh ginger, grated
- [ ] 1 teaspoon turmeric
- [ ] 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- [ ] 1 teaspoon ground coriander
- [ ] 1/2 teaspoon chili powder
- [ ] 2 medium tomatoes, chopped, or 1/2 cup passata
- [ ] 1 cup red split lentils, rinsed
- [ ] 3 cups water
- [ ] 3/4 teaspoon fine salt, plus more to taste
- [ ] 1/2 teaspoon garam masala
- [ ] 1/2 lemon
- [ ] Fresh cilantro, optional

## Instructions

1. Heat the **ghee** over medium heat. Add **cumin seeds**, then **curry leaves**. Fry for 20-30 seconds.
2. Add **onion** and cook for **6-8 minutes**, until golden.
3. Add **garlic and ginger** and cook for about **1 minute**.
4. Add **turmeric, ground cumin, coriander, and chili powder**. Cook for **20-30 seconds**.
5. Add **tomatoes** and cook for **4-6 minutes**, until reduced and jammy.
6. Add **lentils, 3 cups water, and 3/4 teaspoon salt**. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a gentle simmer.
7. Simmer partially covered for **20-25 minutes**, stirring occasionally. Add a splash of water if it gets too thick.
8. Once the lentils are very soft and creamy, stir in **garam masala**.
9. Turn off the heat and add **lemon juice**. Taste and adjust salt; you will likely end up around **1-1 1/4 teaspoons total**.
10. Finish with **cilantro** if using.

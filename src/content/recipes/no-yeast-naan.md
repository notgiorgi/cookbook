A no-yeast Adam Ragusea-style naan dough made with yogurt, baking powder, and baking soda. This amount makes 2 large naan like the video.

## Ingredients

### Dough

- [ ] 2 cups (250 g) all-purpose flour
- [ ] 1 teaspoon sugar
- [ ] 1/2 teaspoon salt
- [ ] 1/4 teaspoon baking soda
- [ ] 1 teaspoon double-acting baking powder
- [ ] 2 tablespoons oil
- [ ] 1/2 cup (100 ml) milk or water, plus more as needed
- [ ] 1/4 cup (60 g) yogurt, ideally with live cultures

### Toppings

- [ ] Grated or chopped garlic
- [ ] Fresh chopped cilantro
- [ ] Melted butter
- [ ] Extra salt, if using unsalted butter

## Instructions

1. Combine all the dough ingredients and knead, adding additional milk or water as needed, until you have a dough that is soft, springy, and only a little sticky.
2. Leave the dough in a big bowl covered with a wet towel. Let it rise for at least 1 hour, but the dough works best if left overnight.
3. Knead the dough again right before baking. Divide it into 2 balls and let them rest for about 15 minutes before rolling them out.
4. Heat a well-seasoned cast iron skillet over medium heat.
5. Roll each dough ball into a large naan. Top with grated or chopped garlic and fresh cilantro, pressing the toppings lightly into the dough so they stick.
6. Cook the naan in the dry skillet until browned in spots and cooked through, flipping as needed.
7. Brush the finished naan with melted butter. If using unsalted butter, finish with a little extra salt.

## Notes

- Add only enough extra liquid to make the dough soft and slightly sticky; the exact amount depends on the flour and yogurt.
- The long rest is mainly for texture and flavor. Yogurt with live cultures may ferment the dough a little overnight.

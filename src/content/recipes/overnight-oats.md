A simple overnight oats base with banana, yogurt, and chia seeds. Top it with anything you like the next day.

## Ingredients

- [ ] 1 cup milk
- [ ] 1 cup oats
- [ ] 1 cup yogurt
- [ ] 1 banana, smashed
- [ ] 1 tablespoon chia seeds
- [ ] 1 pinch salt
- [ ] Cinnamon, to taste

## Instructions

1. Add the milk, oats, yogurt, smashed banana, chia seeds, salt, and cinnamon to a jar or container.
2. Stir well until everything is evenly combined.
3. Cover and refrigerate overnight.
4. The next day, stir again and top with anything you like before eating.

## Notes

- Good topping options include fresh fruit, nuts, seeds, nut butter, honey, maple syrup, jam, or chocolate.
- If you want it looser, add a little more milk the next day.

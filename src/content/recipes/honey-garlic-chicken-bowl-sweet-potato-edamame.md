Caramelized honey garlic chicken with roasted sweet potato, cucumber, edamame, and an optional extra drizzle of honey garlic sauce. Reconstructed from Rachel Johnstone's Honey Garlic Chicken Bowl video.

## Yield
2 servings

## Notes

- The original video identifies the main ingredients but does not specify quantities; these are close reconstructed amounts.
- Boneless chicken thighs are recommended over breast because they caramelize nicely while staying juicy.
- Lower the heat if the honey starts to burn in the pan.

## Ingredients

### Chicken and marinade

- [ ] 350 g boneless chicken thighs, cut into chunks
- [ ] 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- [ ] 1 1/2 tablespoons honey
- [ ] 3 garlic cloves, minced
- [ ] 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
- [ ] 1 tablespoon olive oil
- [ ] 1 teaspoon rice vinegar or lemon juice
- [ ] Black pepper, to taste

### Bowl

- [ ] 1 large sweet potato, about 350 g
- [ ] 150 g shelled edamame
- [ ] 1/2 cucumber, sliced
- [ ] 1 teaspoon olive oil
- [ ] Salt and pepper, to taste

### Optional extra honey garlic sauce

- [ ] 1 tablespoon honey
- [ ] 1 tablespoon soy sauce
- [ ] 1 small garlic clove, minced
- [ ] 2 tablespoons water

## Instructions

### Roast the sweet potato

1. Preheat the oven to 200°C.
2. Slice the sweet potato lengthwise, brush the cut sides with olive oil, and season with salt and pepper.
3. Roast cut-side down for 25 to 35 minutes, until soft and golden.

### Marinate the chicken

4. Mix the soy sauce, honey, garlic, smoked paprika, olive oil, vinegar, and black pepper.
5. Add the chicken and coat thoroughly.
6. Marinate for 10 to 15 minutes.

### Cook the chicken

7. Heat a skillet over medium-high heat.
8. Add the chicken and cook for 8 to 10 minutes, turning until browned and cooked through to 74°C / 165°F internally.

### Prepare the vegetables

9. Boil the frozen shelled edamame according to the package instructions, usually 3 to 5 minutes, then drain.
10. Slice the cucumber.

### Make the optional extra sauce

11. Simmer the honey, soy sauce, garlic, and water in a small saucepan for 1 to 2 minutes, until slightly thickened.

### Assemble

12. Arrange half a roasted sweet potato, cucumber slices, edamame, and glazed chicken in each bowl.
13. Finish with honey garlic sauce if using.

## Estimated nutrition

Approximate per serving, without the optional extra sauce. Values vary with chicken cut, oil, and portion sizes.

- Calories: 590 kcal
- Protein: 40 g
- Carbs: 49 g
- Fat: 26 g

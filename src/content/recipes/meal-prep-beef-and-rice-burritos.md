Spiced rice, saucy ground beef, beans, corn, and cheese wrapped in tortillas. Make a batch to eat now or freeze for later.

Makes about 6 burritos. The video does not specify the meat, tortilla, bean, corn, or cheese quantities; the amounts below are practical estimates for one batch.

## Ingredients

### Rice

- [ ] 1 cup long-grain rice, rinsed
- [ ] 1/2 onion, finely chopped
- [ ] 2 garlic cloves, chopped
- [ ] 35 ml neutral oil, such as corn oil
- [ ] 1 tablespoon tomato paste
- [ ] 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- [ ] 1 stock cube
- [ ] 1.6 cups hot water, measured with the same cup as the rice

### Beef

- [ ] 500 g ground beef
- [ ] 1 onion, roughly chopped
- [ ] 4 garlic cloves, crushed
- [ ] Neutral oil, for the pan
- [ ] Salt, to taste
- [ ] Smoked paprika, generously
- [ ] Hot chili, to taste
- [ ] Garlic powder, to taste
- [ ] 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- [ ] 2 teaspoons dried oregano
- [ ] 2 teaspoons ground cumin
- [ ] 1 heaped tablespoon tomato paste
- [ ] About 1 cup water, plus a splash to loosen the tomato paste

### Assembly

- [ ] About 6 flour tortillas
- [ ] About 1 cup cooked or canned beans, drained
- [ ] About 1 cup corn, drained
- [ ] Mozzarella balls or grated cheddar, about 150 g total

## Instructions

1. Heat the oil for the rice in a saucepan over medium heat. Cook the finely chopped onion and 2 garlic cloves until softened. Stir in 1 tablespoon tomato paste, 1 teaspoon cumin, and the crumbled stock cube.
2. Add the rinsed rice and stir to coat and warm the grains. Pour in the hot water, bring to a boil, cover, and reduce the heat to low. Cook for about 10 minutes, until the water is absorbed. Fluff the rice.
3. While the rice cooks, heat a little oil in a large skillet. Add the beef in spoonfuls and season with salt. Brown it, then add the roughly chopped onion and 4 crushed garlic cloves.
4. Add the smoked paprika, chili, garlic powder, black pepper, oregano, and remaining cumin. Stir in the heaped tablespoon of tomato paste with a splash of water, breaking up the beef. Add about 1 cup more water and simmer gently until the beef is cooked through and the mixture is moist but not runny. Spoon off excess fat if desired.
5. Put some rice and about 2 heaped tablespoons of beef in the center of each tortilla. Add a little cheese, beans, and corn. Fold in the sides, then roll tightly.
6. Toast the burritos seam-side down in a hot skillet with a little oil, turning until golden on both sides and hot in the center.

## Notes

- For a second cheese option, the video uses grated cheddar in some burritos and small mozzarella balls in others.
- To freeze, let the fillings cool, wrap the burritos individually, and freeze. The chef suggests microwaving a frozen burrito for about 2 minutes at medium power, then browning it in a hot skillet. Heat longer as needed until the center is hot.
- The chef also suggests ground chicken, pork, or a beef-pork mix in place of beef.

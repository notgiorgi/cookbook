A quick coconut curry with crisped chickpeas, flexible vegetables, and a lime-balanced finish.

**Serves:** 3-4  
**Time:** ~35 minutes

## Ingredients

- [ ] 1 can chickpeas (400 g / 15 oz), drained and dried
- [ ] 1 can full-fat coconut milk (400 ml)
- [ ] 1 medium onion, diced
- [ ] 3 cloves garlic, minced
- [ ] 1 tablespoon fresh ginger, grated
- [ ] 2-3 tablespoons curry paste, red or yellow
- [ ] 2-3 cups vegetables, such as bell pepper, broccoli, green beans, peas, or spinach
- [ ] 1-2 tablespoons neutral oil
- [ ] 1/2-1 teaspoon salt, to taste
- [ ] 1 teaspoon soy sauce or fish sauce, optional
- [ ] 1/2 teaspoon sugar, optional
- [ ] 1/2 lime
- [ ] Cilantro, optional
- [ ] Rice, to serve

## Instructions

### Crisp the chickpeas

1. Heat about 1 tablespoon oil over medium-high heat.
2. Add the dried chickpeas and a good pinch of salt.
3. Cook for 7-10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until browned and slightly crispy.
4. Remove and set aside.

### Build the curry base

5. Lower the heat to medium and add a little more oil if needed.
6. Add the onion and cook for 4-5 minutes, until softened.
7. Add the curry paste and cook for 1 minute.
8. Add the garlic and ginger and cook for another 30-60 seconds, without letting the garlic burn.

### Add coconut milk

9. Pour in the whole can of coconut milk.
10. Scrape up anything stuck to the pan and bring to a gentle simmer. You do not want the coconut milk to fully break; just simmer it enough for the curry paste and coconut fat to come together.

### Add vegetables

11. Add firm vegetables first, such as broccoli, green beans, or peppers.
12. Simmer for 5-8 minutes.
13. Add quick-cooking vegetables like peas or spinach near the end.

### Return the chickpeas

14. Add the chickpeas back to the pan.
15. Simmer uncovered for another 5-10 minutes, until the sauce reaches the thickness you want.

### Season and serve

16. Add salt to taste.
17. For a more balanced curry, add the soy sauce or fish sauce, sugar, and juice of 1/2 lime. Adjust from there.
18. Serve over rice, optionally with cilantro and another squeeze of lime.

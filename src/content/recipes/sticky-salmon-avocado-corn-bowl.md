A glazed salmon bowl with avocado, sweet corn, cucumber, lime, and fresh herbs. The salad stays light and fresh to balance the sweet-savory salmon glaze.

## Yield
2 servings

## Notes

- The source video does not include exact measurements; these are reconstructed from the visible ingredients.
- Keep the salad dressing simple with olive oil, lime, and herbs rather than a creamy sauce.

## Ingredients

### Salmon

- [ ] 300 g skinless salmon, cut into cubes
- [ ] 1 tablespoon soy sauce
- [ ] 1 tablespoon honey
- [ ] 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
- [ ] 1 garlic clove, minced
- [ ] 1 teaspoon olive oil
- [ ] 1 teaspoon lemon or lime juice

### Salad

- [ ] 2 ears sweet corn, or 200 g canned corn
- [ ] 1 ripe avocado, diced
- [ ] 1 medium cucumber, thinly sliced
- [ ] 2 tablespoons chopped chives or spring onions
- [ ] 2 tablespoons chopped cilantro / coriander
- [ ] 1 tablespoon olive oil
- [ ] Juice of 1 lime
- [ ] Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Instructions

### Marinate the salmon

1. Combine the soy sauce, honey, garlic, smoked paprika, olive oil, and lemon or lime juice.
2. Coat the salmon cubes and leave for 10 minutes.

### Cook the salmon

3. Heat a nonstick pan over medium heat.
4. Cook the salmon for about 3 to 4 minutes per side, until caramelized and cooked through. The honey should create a glossy glaze.

### Prepare the vegetables

5. Cook the corn until tender, then cut the kernels from the cob. If using canned corn, drain well.
6. Thinly slice the cucumber, dice the avocado, and finely chop the herbs.

### Make the salad

7. Mix the corn, cucumber, avocado, chives or spring onions, and cilantro.
8. Add lime juice, olive oil, salt, and black pepper.
9. Toss gently so the avocado stays intact.

### Assemble

10. Transfer the salad to two bowls.
11. Place the sticky salmon cubes on top and finish with a little extra lime juice.
